About Pearlecent

A family dental clinic in Al Rigga, Deira. Trusted team, quality care, continuing our journey since 2018.

Our story

Pearlecent Shifah Medical Center L.L.C. was established in 2018 as Dr. Safar Clinic. Since then it has proudly served the community with dental care and personalised treatment, welcoming more than 7,000 patients.

Our dentists and our team are committed to trusted dental care in a friendly, comfortable and professional environment, helping every patient towards a healthier and more confident smile.

The clinic is in Room 104 on the first floor of the Al Zarooni Building on Al Rigga Road, and stays open until 10 PM, Saturday to Thursday, so a visit fits around work.

A clinic that fits around your day

Established in 2018 as Dr. Safar Clinic, Pearlecent has welcomed more than 7,000 patients from Deira and across Dubai. We are open until 10 PM, six days a week, so a check-up fits around work and family.
